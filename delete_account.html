Duelo de Linhagens: how to delete your account and data

Versão em português

This page explains how to request the deletion of your account in the game Duelo de Linhagens, developed by Duelo de Linhagens (Draco_Astaroth on Google Play).

Option 1: in the game (immediate)

  1. Open Duelo de Linhagens.
  2. In the main menu, tap the Account button (top left corner).
  3. Tap Delete my account.
  4. Tap again to confirm. The account and data are deleted immediately and the game starts over from scratch.

Option 2: by email

  1. Send an email to duelodelinhagens.jogo@gmail.com with the subject Delete my account.
  2. Include your in-game nickname (shown in the Account menu and in the Ranking).
  3. The account and data are deleted within 30 days, and you receive a confirmation by email.

What is deleted

  • The player account (the player code and the link to Google Play Games).
  • The card collection, coins, Essence, decks, daily rewards and quests.
  • The nickname, score and ranking position.
  • The purchase records stored by the game.

What may be kept

  • Google Play purchase receipts stay with Google (not with the game), under Google Play's rules and the law.
  • Ad data collected by Unity Ads follows Unity's policy: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.
  • The anonymous summary of match durations has no link to your account and contains nothing to delete.
  • We keep no copy of your data after deletion.

Warning: deletion cannot be undone. Purchased coins and cards are also lost.
