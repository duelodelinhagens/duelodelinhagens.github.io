Privacy Policy

Duelo de Linhagens · Last updated: October 20, 2026 · Versão em português

This policy explains what data the game Duelo de Linhagens ("the game") uses, why it uses it, and what your rights are. The game is developed by Duelo de Linhagens, which is responsible for processing the data. It follows the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law No. 13,709/2018) and applies to players in every country where the game is available.

1. Summary

  • The game does not ask for or receive your real name, email, phone number or address.
  • Your game account is identified by an automatically created code. On your phone it can be linked to your Google Play Games profile so your progress comes back on another device; in that case we only receive your Play Games player ID, not your email.
  • We store your progress (cards, coins, decks, rewards) in the cloud so it isn't lost.
  • The game shows optional video ads (only when you choose to watch) and sells coins through Google Play.
  • We do not sell your data.

2. Data the game uses

  • Player code (generated by Unity Authentication): to identify your account and store your progress.
  • Google Play Games player ID (only if the account is linked to Play Games): to sign you in automatically and restore your progress and purchases on another device. Your Google account email is not shared with the game.
  • Game progress: card collection, coins, Essence, decks, daily rewards and quests: needed for the game to work; stored in Unity Cloud Save.
  • The nickname you choose and your score: shown in the ranking, visible to other players. Don't use your real name if you don't want to.
  • Connection data (IP address, region) in online matches: to connect you to the other player (Unity Relay and Lobby). We do not store it.
  • Purchase records (Google Play order and receipt code, product purchased): to deliver the coins you bought and avoid duplicate deliveries. We have no access to card or payment data, which stays with Google.
  • Device advertising ID, device model, operating system, language and IP (collected by Unity Ads): to show rewarded videos, check that they were watched and prevent fraud.
  • Match duration and number of turns: added up anonymously into an overall summary (how many matches, average time), not linked to your account. Used to tune the pace of the game.
  • Game milestones: how many installs opened the game, reached each tutorial lesson, played the first match, opened the first pack or came back on another day, counted anonymously (only the total of each milestone, not linked to your account). Used to improve the tutorial and the start of the game.
  • Email and device information, only if you use "Send feedback": the button opens your email app with the game version, phone model and system already filled in; you see everything before sending. We only receive your email address if you send the message, and we use it only to reply.
  • Local settings (volume, language, last deck used): stay only on your device.

3. Third-party services

The game uses services from other companies, which process data under their own policies:

  • Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Cloud Save, Cloud Code, Leaderboards, Relay, Lobby) and Unity Ads, by Unity Technologies: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Google Play and Google Play Games (installation, payments and sign-in), by Google: policies.google.com/privacy

These services may process data outside your country, with the safeguards required by law.

4. Ads

Ads appear only when you tap to watch a video in exchange for a reward. You can limit personalized ads in your Android settings (Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID). The game works normally without watching videos.

5. Notifications

If you allow it, the game schedules a daily reminder of the Journal reward on your device. The reminder is created and shown by your phone, without sending any data to us. You can turn it off at any time in Android's notification settings.

6. Purchases

Coin purchases are made through Google Play's payment system. Refund requests follow Google Play's rules. Coins and items you buy are tied to your game account.

7. How long we keep data

Progress is kept while the account exists. If you ask for deletion, we delete the account and progress within 30 days. Purchase records may be kept for the period required by law.

8. Your rights

You can ask for: confirmation that we process your data, access to it, correction, deletion of the account and data, and information about who it is shared with. To do so, write to duelodelinhagens.jogo@gmail.com with your in-game nickname. You can also delete your account in the game itself, from the Account menu (see how to delete your account).

9. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has sent us data, contact us so it can be deleted.

10. Security

Data is stored on Unity's servers with protected access. Coins and cards can only be changed by the game server, never by the app.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.
